# Astra splat asset provenance

- `astra.vsplat` and `astra-sit.glb` are copies of the character and sitting motion supplied by the project owner, who states these were created on a paid PINOC plan.
- `astra-wave.glb` is the wave motion from `Resources/motions/wave.glb` in https://github.com/jinshuming/visualize-companion. That repository credits PINOC by Viggle for characters and motions but does not provide an explicit license for that motion. Public availability alone does not grant permission. The project owner requested its use after being informed of the unresolved rights; permission for distribution and commercial use still needs verification.
- The rendering library `@viggle/splat-engine` is MIT-licensed, which does not establish rights to the separate character or motion files.

- `astra-sit-alternative.glb` is the `doze.glb` motion from https://github.com/hylu-dev/dream-beyond-the-sky/tree/main/public/assets/anims. The original project describes it as a PINOC motion. Its separate licensing and commercial distribution rights have not been verified; the project owner requested its use after being informed.
